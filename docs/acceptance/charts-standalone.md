# Funnel, Treemap and Sankey

Recharts' charts with their own layouts: `FunnelChart` (through `generateCategoricalChart`, no axes), `Treemap` and
`Sankey` (standalone components with their own layout code, ported from Recharts 2.15.4, not from d3-hierarchy or
d3-sankey). The examples are in `tools/recharts/catalog/hierarchy.mjs`; structure comparisons are against Recharts'
render after mount in Chromium (`tools/recharts/out/<name>.client.html`), as in [charts.md](charts.md).

| Id | Criterion |
|---|---|
| FUNNEL-01 | Given FunnelChart (five items with their own fills, a margin of 30 on the right, a `LabelList` of names at the right), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': each trapezoid's upper width is its value over the largest times the plot width less 50, its lower width the next item's, the last one ends in a point, every trapezoid is inset by 25 px plus Recharts' `top` (which Recharts adds to x), and each label sits 5 px right of the trapezoid's label box. |
| FUNNEL-02 | Given ReversedFunnelChart (`reversed`, a legend, labels at an offset of 20), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the items run bottom to top with their upper and lower widths swapped, the labels keep their unreversed x, the legend has one entry, the data key in the funnel's fill, and the plot's height loses the legend's height twice, as Recharts subtracts it from the offset and again from the funnel's height. |
| FUNNEL-03 | Given funnels whose last value is zero, whose middle value is missing, whose values are all zero, and with no items, when Pictor writes them, then the last zero item keeps its `recharts-funnel-trapezoid` layer without a path (Recharts' `Trapezoid` draws nothing without upper and lower width), a missing value is drawn as 0, all-zero data writes every layer empty instead of failing on the division by the largest value, and no items write no `recharts-trapezoids` layer. |
