# Animations

Recharts 2.15.4's enter animations (`isAnimationActive`, on by default in the browser, with each kind's
`animationBegin`, `animationDuration` and `animationEasing`): on the web as CSS animations in the chart's style block,
inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, no script; on the desktop as frames drawn at the time since
the chart appeared. Chromium's frames are checked by `samples/Demo/animations.mjs` (screenshots at a paused time),
not by these tests.

| Id | Criterion |
|---|---|
| ANIMATION-01 | Given every reference example (each sets `isAnimationActive` to false, as `tools/recharts` renders them), when Pictor writes it, then its markup has no animation class, no animation rule and no `pathLength`: it stays Recharts' static render. |
| ANIMATION-02 | Given SimpleLineChart, DashedLineChart, SimpleAreaChart, PositiveAndNegativeBarChart, VerticalBarChart and SimpleScatterChart with their series animated (Recharts' defaults), when Pictor writes them, then each series' layer is marked `pictor-a`; an undashed line's curve is drawn (`pictor-draw`, `pathLength="1"`), a dashed one is drawn through a mask of its path (`pictor-dash-mask`, ANIMATION-20) and an area's shapes are revealed (`pictor-reveal`); a bar grows from its base (`pictor-grow-b` above it, `pictor-grow-t` below, `pictor-grow-l` and `pictor-grow-r` in a vertical chart); a scatter's symbols grow by area (`pictor-scale-area`); dots, label lists and error bars wait for the end (`pictor-late`); a bar's layer carries `--pd:400ms` and a scatter's `--pe:linear`; and every rule is inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference)`; on the desktop, 100 ms into SimpleScatterChart's movement (a quarter of its linear 400 ms, after its 80 ms start), each symbol covers between 12% and 40% of its resting area (a quarter, its area growing linearly as Recharts interpolates `size`; growing by radius it would cover a sixteenth). |
| ANIMATION-03 | Given TwoLevelPieChart, StraightAnglePieChart, SimpleRadarChart, SimpleRadialBarChart, FunnelChart, SimpleTreemap and SimpleSankey animated, when Pictor writes them, then a pie's layer sweeps counterclockwise from its start angle around its center after 400 ms (`pictor-sweep`, `--cx`, `--cy`, `--f:90deg`, `--n:360deg`, `--pb:400ms`) and a pie from 180° to 0° clockwise (`pictor-sweep-cw`, `--n:180deg`); a radar's polygon grows from the chart's center (`pictor-center`, its `transform-origin`); each ring grows by one arc of its color (`pictor-arc`, a straight-angle pie's from 0 over 180°: `--a:0;--b:0;--l:180`); each radial bar sweeps by its own angle; a funnel's trapezoids grow from their centers after 400 ms; a treemap's nodes slide in from offsets that are the same on every render, linearly over 1500 ms; and a Sankey, which Recharts does not animate, has no animation. |
| ANIMATION-04 | Given a line with `AnimationBegin` 200, `AnimationDuration` 800 and `AnimationEasing` `EaseOut`, and a second line with the defaults, when Pictor writes the chart, then the first line's layer carries `--pb:200ms;--pd:800ms;--pe:ease-out`, the second none, and the chart's markup holds the drawing rule once. |
| ANIMATION-05 | Given SimpleBarChart animated on the desktop with a test clock, when it is drawn at 0 ms, 280 ms and 512 ms, then no bar is drawn at 0 ms, the first bar is drawn from its base to 80% of its height at 280 ms (CSS's `ease` at half the time, after Recharts' 80 ms start: react-smooth reaches its first frame five animation frames after the mount), and at 512 ms (the end, plus the two frames after which Recharts draws what waits for the end) the chart is drawn as the static chart is, pixel for pixel. |
| ANIMATION-06 | Given SimpleLineChart animated on the desktop with a test clock, when it is drawn at 830 ms, then its lines are drawn only part of their length and none of its dots is drawn; when it is drawn at 1612 ms, then it is drawn as the static chart is. |
| ANIMATION-07 | Given an animated chart in a window whose `Chart.ReduceMotion` is set, when it is drawn at 0 ms, then it is drawn as the static chart is (the app's switch for the operating system's reduced-motion preference). |
| ANIMATION-08 | Given an animated chart that has finished its enter animation, when its data is replaced by items with the same values, then it does not animate again: drawn at the time of the change it shows its bars at rest, as Recharts' update finds every point equal to the previous one (lodash's `isEqual`); new values play the update animation (UPDATE-01..15), never the enter animation. |
| ANIMATION-09 | Given TwoLevelPieChart and StraightAnglePieChart animated on the desktop, when they appear, then every frame draws each sector as Recharts' frame at the same time after the mount (recorded in Chromium): after the 400 ms delay and Recharts' 80 ms start, every sector grows from 0 to its angle at once, packed from the first sector's start angle with the padding before every sector but the first (Recharts' update from an empty `prevSectors`), its labels waiting for the end. |
| ANIMATION-10 | Given SimpleRadarChart animated on the desktop, when it appears, then every frame draws its polygon as Recharts' frame at the same time: each point comes from the center, its dots with it. |
| ANIMATION-11 | Given SimpleRadialBarChart animated on the desktop, when it appears, then every frame draws each sector as Recharts' frame at the same time: its end angle sweeps from its start angle. |
| ANIMATION-12 | Given TwoLevelPieChart and PieChartWithPaddingAngle animated, when Pictor writes them for the web, then they hold no mask: their sectors are grown, while the pie animates in, by arcs of their colors at their middle radius and as wide as them (`pictor-arc`, a path length of 360 whose dash moves from `--a` to `--b` degrees and grows to `--l` by the pie's eased progress), the white edges turning with the arcs' ends, and the sectors themselves wait for the end; at 700, 1100 and 1600 ms each arc starts at its first sector's start angle in the desktop's frame (and so in Recharts') and spans exactly the angles of the sectors it grows (all of a one-color ring, one sector of a padded pie, clockwise or counterclockwise), and a radial bar's sectors are grown by arcs the same way. |
| ANIMATION-13 | Given RadarOptions (a radar with dots) animated, when Pictor writes it for the web, then each dot comes from the chart's center with its point (`pictor-converge`, `--dx` and `--dy` the center less the dot) instead of waiting for the end, as Recharts draws a radar's dots at its animated points. |

| ANIMATION-16 | Given every movement's rules, the base rules and a `ReplayButton`'s alternates, when Pictor writes them for the web, then each movement keeps its effect only while it runs: every `animation` shorthand fills `backwards` or not at all (none fills `both` or `forwards`, none is `infinite`), the arcs' and edges' included, so the chart at rest is its static markup and no finished animation stays in the document's animations (`document.getAnimations()` is empty once the enter has ended on /pie and /radial-bar, in Chromium and Firefox: `samples/Demo/pie-perf.mjs`); the arcs and edges are hidden at rest and under reduced motion by their group (`.pictor-shown{visibility:hidden}` outside the media query), so the static sectors show. |
