# Interactions

Legend toggles (a Pictor feature: Recharts leaves a legend's clicks to the app) and Recharts' `Brush`. A toggled
series is hidden without laying the chart out again, the same on both targets; the brush is dragged on the desktop and
moved on the web through two range inputs posted as the framework's fields, whose answer is the chart over the new
window.

| Id | Criterion |
|---|---|
| LEGEND-01 | Given SimpleLineChart with `Legend Toggles="True"`, when Pictor writes it, then each legend item holds a `label` with a hidden checkbox (`pictor-t0`, `pictor-t1`) inside Recharts' `li`, each series' layer is wrapped in `pictor-s0` or `pictor-s1`, each tooltip entry and active dot carries its series' class, and the style block hides a series (`.recharts-wrapper:has(.pictor-tK:checked) .pictor-sK{display:none}`) and draws its legend item in Recharts' inactive `#ccc`; without toggles the markup holds no checkbox and no series class, as Recharts' render. |
| LEGEND-02 | Given a line chart with toggles on the desktop, when the `uv` legend item is clicked, then the scene is drawn without the `uv` line, its legend item in `#ccc`, the hovered tooltip without its entry and the y domain unchanged; when it is clicked again, the line is back; a click beside the items toggles nothing. |
| LEGEND-03 | Given polar charts with toggles (TwoLevelPieChart's legend lists sectors, SpecifiedDomainRadarChart's lists radars), when Pictor writes them, then each sector (in every pie that draws it) and each radar is wrapped in its entry's class with its checkbox; a responsive chart's legend inside the SVG holds its checkboxes in foreign objects (AUDIT-48). |
