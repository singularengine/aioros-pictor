# Motion and input

Recharts 2.15.4's update animations (each series moving from its previous geometry to its new one when the chart's
data changes), its touch handling and its keyboard navigation (`accessibilityLayer`), and the chart's accessible
description. Recharts' frames are recorded in Chromium by `tools/recharts/motion.mjs` (Playwright's clock driving the
animation frames every 16 ms) and compared frame for frame with Pictor's update at the same time after Recharts' start
within 0.01 px, at the same time after the update: Recharts' animation starts five animation frames after the change
(80 ms at the reference's 16 ms frames: react-smooth's manager passes `onAnimationStart`, the begin delay and the start
of its frame loop through `requestAnimationFrame`) and draws what waits for the end two frames after its last frame,
and Pictor's timing includes both on the two targets.

## Touch

Recharts on a touch screen: a tap shows the state under the finger (the browser's compatibility mouse events reach
the chart's `onMouseEnter`/`onMouseMove`, or an item's `onMouseEnter`), dragging the finger moves an axis tooltip
(`handleTouchMove`, the wrapper's touch handler of charts whose tooltip follows the axis) while an item's tooltip stays
(items take no touch events), lifting the finger keeps the tooltip (`handleTouchEnd` only reports `onMouseUp`), and a
tap elsewhere hides it (the wrapper's `mouseleave`). The desktop is driven with touch pointers in Avalonia's headless
platform; the web's tap is checked in Chromium with touch emulation against Recharts driven by the same taps
(`samples/Demo/touch.mjs`). On the web a drag moves an axis tooltip through Aioros's touch follow: the hit layer of a
chart whose tooltip follows the axis carries `data-aioros-follow`, the framework's script focuses the hit group under
the finger on `touchstart` and `touchmove`, and the style block shows the state of the group with visible focus (the
script's focus after a touch matches `:focus-visible` in Chromium and Firefox, a mouse click's does not). Checked in
Chromium (CDP touch events) and Firefox 157 (WebDriver BiDi touch actions) against Recharts driven by the same touches.

## Keyboard

Recharts 2.15.4's `accessibilityLayer` (off by default): the surface is focusable (`tabIndex` 0, `role="application"`),
focus shows the active category's tooltip (the default index, or the first) and `ArrowRight` and `ArrowLeft` move it
within the categories, clamped, through its `AccessibilityManager`, which only acts in a horizontal chart whose tooltip
follows the axis (it returns at once in a vertical, polar or funnel chart, and a spoofed pointer cannot enter a
scatter's items); no other key moves it. Its tooltip's `Escape` (any chart) hides the tooltip's box until the tooltip
moves. Checked against Recharts in Chromium and Firefox by `samples/Demo/keyboard.mjs`. On the web the arrows move
the focus among the chart's hit groups through Aioros's roving focus (`data-aioros-roving="horizontal"`), each group's
visible focus showing its state, with one tab stop for the chart.
