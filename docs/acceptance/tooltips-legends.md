# Tooltips, legends, labels, shapes and responsive sizes

Recharts' `Tooltip`, `Legend`, `LabelList`, the shape props of series, grids and cells, and `ResponsiveContainer`,
drawn by Pictor from reference examples (`tools/recharts/catalog/tips.mjs`) and compared with Recharts 2.15.4's render
in Chromium. A hover or click state is compared with Recharts' render after the same pointer events
(`tools/recharts/out/<name>.<i>.client.html`): Pictor's hit testing finds the state under the pointer, and its hidden
tip and cursor in the web markup must match Recharts' visible tooltip (its position, label and lines with their colors),
cursor, active dots and active bars (`HoverComparison`). Positions match within 0.01 px, or within 0.5 px when the box is
flipped or placed by its measured size (Recharts measures it in the DOM, Pictor with Inter's metrics).
