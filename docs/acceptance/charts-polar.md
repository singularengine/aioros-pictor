# Polar charts

Recharts' polar charts (`PieChart`, `RadarChart`, `RadialBarChart`), drawn by Pictor from Recharts' own examples
(`tools/recharts/catalog/polar.mjs`, from the Recharts 2.x site's `src/docs/exampleComponents`) and compared with
Recharts 2.15.4's render after mount in Chromium (`tools/recharts/out/<name>.client.html`, Inter 14 px), element for
element as the cartesian charts are (`MarkupComparison`, see [charts.md](charts.md)). A radial bar chart is compared with
Recharts 3.8.0's render instead (`tools/recharts/out/recharts3/<name>.client.html`, written by `recharts3.mjs`), the
version shadcn/ui's charts use (decisions).

| Id | Criterion |
|---|---|
| POLAR-01 | Given TwoLevelPieChart (an inner pie of radius 60 and an outer ring from 70 to 90, each with its own data, the ring labelled), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': each pie's sectors run counterclockwise from 0° in proportion to their values around the center of the plot, and each ring sector has a label line from its outer edge 20 px outwards and its value at the line's end, anchored away from the center, in the sector's color; the label straight below the center, whose x is a hair left of it (199.99999999999997), is anchored at its end, as Recharts compares the exact doubles. |
| POLAR-02 | Given StraightAnglePieChart (a pie from 180° to 0°, labelled), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the sectors run clockwise over the upper half only, and the labels right of the center are anchored at their start, those left of it at their end. |
| POLAR-03 | Given TwoSimplePieChart (a pie at a percentage center and a donut at a center given in pixels, a tooltip), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': a center in pixels is measured from the plot's left and top (5 px of default margin in), and the chart carries one hover state per sector of both pies (each sector's transparent hit shape and its hidden tooltip with the sector's name and value). |
| POLAR-04 | Given PieChartWithPaddingAngle (two donuts with a 5° padding angle and a `Cell` color per sector), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': each sector after the first starts 5° after the previous one ends, the sectors share what is left of the sweep, and each takes its cell's fill. |
| POLAR-05 | Given ActiveShapePieChart (a donut with `ActiveIndex` 1 and an active shape of outer radius 90 and fill `#ff7300`), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the active sector is drawn in place with the active shape's props merged over its own, inside Recharts' `recharts-active-shape` layer; and given a pie with an active shape but no active index, when it is written, then no sector is drawn active and each sector's hover state holds its active shape. |
| POLAR-06 | Given SimpleRadarChart (a polygon grid, an angle axis of six subjects, a radius axis and one radar), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the subjects sit every 60° from the top, clockwise, the radius axis' nice ticks 0 to 120 draw concentric polygons, its tick labels are rotated along the axis at 0°, and the radar's polygon closes through its six points. |
| POLAR-07 | Given SpecifiedDomainRadarChart (a radius axis at 30° with the domain 0 to 150, two radars, a legend and a tooltip), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the radius axis' ticks are recharts-scale's 0, 40, 80 and 150 for that fixed domain, the plot's radius shrinks by the legend's measured height, and the legend lists both radars with rectangle icons. |
| POLAR-08 | Given SimpleRadialBarChart (seven radial bars of size 10 with backgrounds, labels inside their start along the arc, data colors, a vertical legend at the right middle), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the bars run from 0° over the angle axis' nice domain, sit in the radius axis' bands at Recharts' bar position, each label follows its bar's arc on a text path, and the vertical legend takes its measured width from the plot's right and sits at the chart's vertical middle. |
| POLAR-09 | Given a polar chart's spec, when it is read back from the JSON the web plan records, then it writes the same markup: a pie's own data travels inside the spec as a table, and a pie without data reads the chart's data. |
| POLAR-10 | Given a radar chart and a radial bar chart with a tooltip, when the web writer writes them, then each category has a hover state (a hit sector around its angle with the axis cursor line, or a hit ring around its band with Recharts' sector cursor), and hidden tips with the radars' active dots and the tooltip listing each series; and given a chart without a tooltip, then it has no hover state at all. |
| POLAR-11 | Given SimpleRadarChart and TwoLevelPieChart built from their XAML elements, when the desktop control draws them (Avalonia, Skia, Inter at 14 px), then the frame keeps the screenshot's size and fewer of its inked pixels than the example's limit (ORACLE-03) differ by more than 64 in a channel from Recharts' screenshot in Chromium. |
| POLAR-12 | Given TwoSimplePieChart in a window, when the pointer moves over the donut's fourth sector, then the hovered index is that sector's (the first pie's six sectors come first) and the chart draws its tooltip; when the pointer moves to the chart's empty corner, then nothing is hovered. |
| POLAR-13 | Given RadialBarOptions (a `maxBarSize` of 14, a corner radius of 10 with `forceCornerRadius` and `cornerIsExternal`, and a `background` object with its own fill, stroke and stroke width), when Pictor writes it, then its markup matches Recharts': the bars no wider than 14 px, corners rounding outwards, a bar too narrow for its corners drawn as Recharts' rounded cap, the background sectors with the object's paints. |
| POLAR-14 | Given StackedRadialBarChart (two `RadialBar`s of one `stackId`), when Pictor writes it, then the bars share each band and the second starts where the first ends, over the angle axis' domain of the stacked sums, as Recharts stacks them. |
| POLAR-15 | Given RadialBarItemTooltip (`shared={false}`), when the pointer enters a bar, then Pictor's hover state of that bar matches Recharts 3's: no cursor, the box at the chart's top left corner (Recharts 3's `RadialBar` gives an item tooltip no position, so its wrapper is not translated), one line with the bar's value. |
| POLAR-16 | Given PieActiveShapes (`activeIndex={[0, 2]}`, an active shape, an inactive shape of fill opacity 0.5 and `blendStroke`), when Pictor writes it, then sectors 0 and 2 are drawn with the active shape in Recharts' active layer, the others with the inactive shape's props, every stroke the sector's fill, as Recharts renders them. |
| POLAR-17 | Given PieLabelProps (a `label` object with an offset radius of 30, a fill, a size and a weight, and a `labelLine` object with a stroke, a width and dashes), when Pictor writes it, then the labels and their lines carry those props, as Recharts renders them. |
| POLAR-18 | Given PieAngles (a pie from 90° to -270° with a padding angle of 3 and a minimum angle of 10 that widens a small sector), when Pictor writes it, then its sectors and labels match Recharts': the sectors run clockwise, each separated by 3°, the small one at least 10° wide. |
| POLAR-19 | Given RadialBarOptions, StackedRadialBarChart, PieActiveShapes and PieLabelProps built from their XAML elements (`MaxBarSize`, `StackId`, `ForceCornerRadius`, `CornerIsExternal`, `Background*`, `ActiveIndexes`, `InactiveShape`, `BlendStroke`, `Label*` and `LabelLine*`), when the desktop control records its spec, then the web writer writes from it the same markup as from Recharts' props. |
| POLAR-20 | Given the radial bar charts of the catalog `recharts3.mjs` renders (19 of its 22, the three whose shapes or labels are templates being compared by the custom content criteria), when Pictor writes it, then its drawn elements match Recharts 3.8.0's client render class by class and in document order within a class, Recharts 3's z-index layers left out: the rings from their band's start (`getCateCoordinateOfBar` on `combineAxisTicks`' ticks, half a band in), each series' own domain where radial bars stack and `[0, 1]` with `stackOffset="expand"` (zero-size sectors not drawn), an angle axis' domain neither cut by `allowDataOverflow` nor extended by its nice ticks, its ticks without the duplicate of 0° at 360° and their labels anchored vertically (`getTickTextVerticalAnchor`: 0.71em in the top quarter, 0 in the bottom one, 0.355em at the sides), the radius axis' category ticks at their band's middle, the plot shrunk by the legend's unrounded height, the legend listing every radial bar's data entries (a hidden one's too) by their `name` and `fill` fields sorted by name (`itemSorter="value"`), the label lists running counterclockwise whatever their `clockWise`; the comparison fails on a drawn element missing, added or moved. |
